GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MyBroker free for property buyers?
Yes, 100% free. Buyers never pay anything. We earn from broker subscriptions. You search, compare, and meet brokers at zero cost — forever.
How are brokers verified?
Every broker goes through 3-step verification: (1) Identity check — Aadhaar & PAN verified in person, (2) Reference check — we call 3-5 past clients, (3) Experience audit — deal history verified. Only those who pass all three get listed.
What is the Trust Score?
Trust Score is our proprietary rating (0-100) calculated from: verification status (10%), years of experience (25%), deals completed (20%), client ratings (30%), and review count (15%). It updates monthly based on real performance data.
Can I meet multiple brokers?
Absolutely! We encourage meeting 2-3 brokers before deciding. Each meeting is a no-obligation consultation arranged by us.
How does MyBroker make money?
Brokers pay a monthly or yearly subscription to be listed. Premium plans offer more visibility and leads. Buyers never pay — our revenue is 100% from broker subscriptions.
Is my information safe?
Your phone number is never shared with brokers until you request a meeting. Even then, we make the introduction call first. No broker can see your details directly.
Which areas in Surat are covered?
We currently cover 9 major areas: Vesu, Adajan, Pal, Althan, City Light, Athwa, Piplod, Dumas, and Katargam — expanding monthly.
